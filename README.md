# Art Century

**Art Century** is a creative online platform dedicated to art, culture, music, and education. Its purpose is to bring together people who are passionate about creativity, provide inspiration, and make art more accessible.

The platform combines an online art gallery, editorial articles, music discoveries, educational resources, and creative tools in one place.

---

## 1. Project Overview

- **Project name:** Art Century
- **Project type:** Responsive single-page website
- **Main purpose:** To create an online space for discovering, exploring, and sharing art.
- **Target platform:** Web browsers
- **Project status:** Initial development

Art Century aims to become a digital community where artists, students, creative professionals, and art enthusiasts can discover new perspectives and share their knowledge.

## 2. Target Audience

The website is designed for:

- Art enthusiasts interested in different forms of art.
- Artists, painters, musicians, and other creative professionals.
- Students studying art, music, design, or related disciplines.
- People looking for creative inspiration.
- Users searching for art schools, studios, and educational institutions.
- Writers and contributors interested in publishing articles about art.

## 3. Functional Requirements

The website should provide the following functionality:

### 3.1. Navigation

- Navigation between the main website sections.
- Smooth scrolling to selected sections.
- Responsive navigation menu for mobile devices.
- Search interface for discovering website content.

### 3.2. Articles

- Display articles related to art and culture.
- Organize articles into categories.
- Provide article previews with images, titles, authors, and publication dates.
- Allow users to explore different topics.

Planned functionality:
- User registration and authentication.
- Article submission and publication.
- Author profiles and content management.

### 3.3. Paintings

- Display a collection of paintings and artworks.
- Provide artwork titles and artist information.
- Organize artworks into a visual gallery.
- Allow users to explore different artistic styles.

### 3.4. Music

- Display music collections and playlists.
- Provide information about musical works and genres.
- Include interactive playback controls.

Future functionality may include audio streaming and music recommendations.

### 3.5. Education

- Display art schools, music schools, universities, and creative studios.
- Provide information about educational institutions.
- Allow users to filter institutions by discipline and study format.
- Support searching for educational opportunities.

### 3.6. Creative Apps

- Present applications and digital tools for artists and musicians.
- Provide descriptions and links to external resources.
- Organize tools by creative category.

### 3.7. About

- Provide information about the Art Century project.
- Explain the platform's purpose and concept.
- Include contact information and community guidelines.

### 3.8. User Contributions

The platform is planned to support community contributions.

Registered users will be able to submit articles, share their perspectives, and contribute educational and cultural content.

## 4. Website Structure

The initial version is implemented as a single-page website with the following sections:

| Section | Description |
|---|---|
| Header | Logo, navigation, search, and user actions |
| Hero | Introduction to the platform |
| Quick Links | Shortcuts to the main sections |
| Articles | Editorial content about art |
| Paintings | Visual art gallery |
| Music | Music collections and playlists |
| Education | Directory of educational institutions |
| Apps | Creative applications and tools |
| About / Footer | Project information, contacts, and additional links |

## 5. Design Requirements

The visual identity is based on a minimalist editorial aesthetic inspired by contemporary art magazines.

**Color palette:**
- Soft pink — `#F9DFF1`
- Off-white — `#FFF9FC`
- Light lavender — `#F3E8F4`
- Dark plum — `#352039`
- Accent pink — `#E9A3DF`

**Typography:**
- Playfair Display — headings and editorial titles.
- DM Sans — body text, navigation, and interface elements.

**Design principles:**
- Minimalist and elegant visual presentation.
- Large artwork images and visual storytelling.
- Generous whitespace.
- Clear navigation and content hierarchy.
- Rounded buttons and cards.
- Subtle transitions and scroll-based animations.
- Consistent visual identity across all sections.

The website design is based on the original Art Century Figma mockup.

## 8. Future Development

Potential improvements and planned features include:

- User registration and authentication.
- Author profiles and article publishing.
- Individual pages for articles and artworks.
- Advanced search and content filtering.

## 9. Project Goal

The main goal of Art Century is to create an accessible and visually engaging digital environment where art, music, education, and creative ideas come together.

**Art is not just something we observe. It is something we experience, discover, and share.**
